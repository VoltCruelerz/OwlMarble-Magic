## Watervall v Agile

- waterfall: theory/plan-driven
  - silos, handoff between sequences (plan/create/review/release)
  - upstream changes require CAB (change approval board)
  - scope fixed, time/cost variable
  - quality ambiguous until end
- agile: adaptive/change-driven
  - iteration of 1 month or less (sprints)
  - prelim outcome reviewed by customer
  - scope dynamic, time/cost fixed
  - quality predictable along the way
  - incremental improvement usable along the way (MVP should be usable)
  - Iterations
    - Minimum Viable Product: barely usable by early customers who provide feedback, eliminate waste
    - Minimum Marketable Product: 
    - Minimum Lovable Product:

### Scope

- **can** be clarified and renegotiated with the product owner as more is learned

### Other

- the Product Owner can cancel a sprint
  - can do so when sprint goal is obsolete
- burndown chart theoretically updated during daily scrum
  - burndown chart not required
  - but org **must** use some kind of monitoring
- scope can be renegotiated

### Sprint Planning

- sprint planning duration: 2*(weeks per sprint) hours
- addresses
  - why is sprint valuable
  - what can be done
  - how will it be done
- output is the backlog
  - backlog = goal, delivery plan, PBIs
- should include team (SM, PO, devs)
  - can invite others for expertise

### Daily Scrum

- if PO or SM are working, can attend as devs
  - must take off PO/SM hat for meeting
  - teams are self-organized
  - assume competence
  - **PO must not be SM**
- improve communication
- identify impediments
  - do not solve them
- devs can replan outside meeting
- devs can use whatever structure and techniqeues they want as long as the daily scrum focuses on progress and an actionable plan
  - encourages self-management

### Burndown

- "above" schedule = behind schedule
- "below" schedule = ahead of schedule

### Sprint Review

- duration = #weeks in hours,
  - 4 hours of review per month
- inspect outcome of sprint and determine future adaptations
- team presents results to stakeholders
- review what was accomplished in the sprint
- collaborate on what to do next

### Sprint Retro

- duration = 0.75 hours * weeks
  - 3 hours per month
- team plans ways to improve quality/effectiveness
- inspect last review
  - individuals
  - interactions
  - processes
  - tools
  - Definition of Done
- how problems were or were not solved
- solutions to problem may create tickets at top of product backlog

### Who Goes When

- "Sprint X": whole team (devs/PO/SM)
- "Scrum": devs only (if SM/PO are devs, they attend as devs)

### Cone of Uncertainty

- theme -> epic -> story -> task
  - theme: top level objective or vision
  - epics: high level functions
  - stories: the story with INVESTment
    - can be finished in 1 sprint
  - task: sub-tasks of story
- expresses uncertainty in the best case scenario
  - eg no scope changes
  - from 0.25x to 4x to what will actually happen
- can make promises later on as a product owner
  - should avoid them early on because too risky

## Scrum Artifacts

### Product Backlog

- emergent ordered list of what is needed to improve
- single source of work to be undertaken by the team
- PBIs that can be done in one sprint can be selected in a Sprint Planning event
- refining it is not an event!
  - happens in backgroun by PO
  - <10% of time per week
- may only have one product goal at a time

### Sprint Backlog

- created in sprint planning
- pull the PBIs
- create actionable plan
- owned (aka accountable) by the developers
- if have to rescope, renegotiate with PO with Sprint Goal in mind

### Increment

- cahracteristics:
  - done
  - usable?
  - potentially releasable
- individual stable stepping stone
- eg partial breaking changes are *not* increments.
  - would need to update both ATGS and ATSP
- can have multiple in a sprint
- **the moment a PBI meets the DoD, an Increment is born**
- If a PBI does not meet DoD, it is not presented at Sprint Review
- DoD defined by the organization if it exists
  - otherwise created by the team
  - if multiple teams working on same product, must share DoD
